Privacy Policy

App: Arrow Way: Puzzle Adventure

Effective Date: October 6, 2026  |  Last Updated: October 6, 2026

Summary: Arrow Way is an offline-friendly casual logic puzzle game. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal identity information (such as your real name, email, phone number, or contacts). Third-party services like Google AdMob and Google Play Services collect anonymized telemetry and device identifiers to provide ads, cloud save, and in-app purchases.

1. Information We Collect and Receive

Arrow Way does not require you to create a proprietary account or provide personal identifiable information (PII) to play.

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Game Progress: Unlocked levels, stars, hearts, daily challenge streaks, and preferences (sound, haptics, theme). This data is stored strictly on your local device via Android DataStore.

B. Information Collected by Third-Party Partners

To enable core gameplay features, advertisements, and optional cloud backups, we integrate certified Google Play services:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): Used to display banner advertisements and user-initiated rewarded video ads (for free lives or hints). AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, and ad interaction diagnostics to deliver personalized and non-personalized advertising in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies. For details, visit Google AdMob Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Games Services (Cloud Save & Leaderboards): If you choose to connect your Google Play Games account, your display name and game snapshot progress are synced securely to Google Cloud. You may disconnect at any time. For details, visit Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases): Used to process one-time optional purchases (such as "Remove Ads"). All payments are processed directly and securely by Google Play. We never receive, process, or store your credit card or financial information.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate and maintain the puzzle game mechanics and track your level progress.
  • To allow you to optionally restore your game progress across multiple devices via Google Cloud Snapshots.
  • To provide voluntary rewarded video ads that grant optional in-game hints and revives.
  • To verify in-app purchases and remove advertisements for premium supporters.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties. Data is only processed through official Google SDKs as described above in strict compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Your local gameplay data remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the application. You can also reset all progress directly in the in-app Settings menu at any time. For Google Play Games Cloud Saves, you may manage and delete saved game snapshots directly through the Google Play Games app on your Android device.

5. Children's Privacy

Arrow Way is designed for general audiences of all ages and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in the European Union). Our ad requests are configured to comply with Google Play's Families Policy and COPPA regulations.

6. Security

We prioritize your privacy and data security. All external network communications (Google AdMob, Play Games, and Play Billing) occur exclusively over encrypted HTTPS/TLS protocols.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Compliance)

Depending on your location, you may have rights under regional privacy laws such as the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Opt-out of Personalized Ads: You can reset or opt out of personalized ad tracking anytime on your Android device via Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • Data Access / Erasure: Because we do not store identifying account data on private servers, you retain complete control over your data directly on your device.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game updates or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
